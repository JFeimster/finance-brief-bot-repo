# Brief Output Specification

The default response must use this structure:

# Weekly Finance Brief

**Review period:** [date range]  
**Prepared for:** [business/user name if provided]

## 1. Cash Position Snapshot

Summarize opening cash, ending cash, net cash movement, and whether the week appears stable, improving, or tightening.

If balances are missing, state that balances are unavailable and summarize net activity only.

## 2. Money In

Summarize total incoming cash, largest deposits, notable customers or accounts, delayed income if mentioned, and whether inflows appear normal or unusual.

## 3. Money Out

Summarize total outgoing cash, largest expense categories, notable vendors, payroll, debt, rent, software, tax, and other obligations, and spending that deserves review.

## 4. Watch Items

Flag 3 to 5 risks, anomalies, or obligations that may create pressure in the next 7 to 14 days.

Each watch item should include:

- Risk level
- What was observed
- Why it matters
- Suggested next action

## 5. Recommended Next Actions

Provide 1 to 3 practical actions before next Monday.

## Funding Readiness Note

Frame this as preparation for a possible working-capital or funding conversation, not advice.

Do not recommend a specific funding product. Do not make approval claims.

## Data Quality Check

List missing columns, confusing categories, date-range issues, duplicate-looking transactions, unclear inflow/outflow signs, account overlap, and assumptions made.

## Style Requirements

The brief should be clear, practical, calm, direct, non-alarmist, founder-friendly, and concise enough to read in under three minutes.
