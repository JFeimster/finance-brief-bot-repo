# Cash Flow Calculation Rules

## Core Metrics

Calculate:

- Total inflows
- Total outflows
- Net cash movement
- Largest incoming transactions
- Largest outgoing transactions
- Major spending categories
- Possible recurring obligations

## Inflows

Include customer payments, sales deposits, processor payouts, refunds received, loan proceeds if clearly identified, and owner contributions if clearly identified.

Separate operating revenue from non-operating inflows when possible.

## Outflows

Include payroll, contractor payments, rent or facilities, software subscriptions, marketing and advertising, debt service, taxes, inventory or COGS, insurance, professional services, travel and meals, and owner draws or distributions.

## Transfers

Transfers should not be treated as revenue or expense when they clearly move cash between accounts belonging to the same business.

If transfer status is unclear, flag it.

## Net Cash Movement

Formula:

Total Inflows - Total Outflows = Net Cash Movement

If balances are present, compare net movement to the change in balance. If the two do not reconcile, mention this in the Data Quality Check.

## Cash Position

If reliable balances exist:

- Opening cash = balance at or near beginning of review period
- Ending cash = balance at or near end of review period

If reliable balances do not exist:

- State that cash position cannot be determined from the uploaded file.
