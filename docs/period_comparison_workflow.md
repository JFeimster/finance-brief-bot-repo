# Period Comparison Workflow

When several weeks or periods are uploaded, compare periods and identify trends.

## Compare

- Total inflows
- Total outflows
- Net cash movement
- Payroll
- Contractors
- Rent or facilities
- Software
- Marketing
- Debt service
- Taxes
- Large vendor payments
- Owner draws or distributions

## Trend Language

Use plain-English wording:

- "Inflows increased compared with the prior period."
- "Outflows rose mainly because of..."
- "Cash flow tightened because..."
- "The largest change was..."
- "Trend analysis is limited because..."

## When Prior Data Is Missing

If only one week is provided, do not imply a trend.

Say:

"Trend analysis requires additional weeks of data. This brief focuses only on the uploaded week."
