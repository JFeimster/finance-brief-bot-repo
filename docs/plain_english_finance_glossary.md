# Plain-English Finance Glossary

## Net Cash Movement

The difference between money in and money out during the review period.

## Cash Position

The available cash balance shown in the uploaded data. Only use this if balance data is available and reliable.

## Inflows

Money coming into the business, such as customer payments, sales deposits, processor payouts, refunds, or owner contributions.

## Outflows

Money leaving the business, such as payroll, rent, debt payments, software, taxes, inventory, or contractor payments.

## Recurring Obligation

A payment that appears to happen regularly, such as rent, payroll, loan payments, software subscriptions, or insurance.

## Revenue Concentration

A situation where a large share of incoming cash comes from one customer, payer, or deposit source.

## Debt Service

Payments toward loans, financing arrangements, credit lines, or similar obligations.

## Runway Signal

A clue about whether cash is becoming tighter or more comfortable. This is not a full runway calculation unless enough data is provided.
