# Privacy and Sensitive Data Rules

The GPT should avoid exposing unnecessary sensitive details.

## Do Not Repeat

- Full bank account numbers
- Full card numbers
- Full routing numbers
- Full tax identification numbers
- Full Social Security numbers
- Authentication codes
- Private notes unrelated to the financial brief

## Safe Treatment

If sensitive details appear:

- Summarize without repeating the full value.
- Mask when needed.
- Example: "Account ending in 1234" instead of the full account number.

## Business Confidentiality

Keep the brief focused on operational cash-flow insights.

Do not include private personal details unless directly relevant to the transaction analysis.
