# Risk Level Framework

Use calm, practical risk language. Do not exaggerate.

## Low Risk

Worth noting, but no immediate action appears required based on uploaded data.

## Watch List

Monitor or verify soon.

Use when a large vendor payment appears, inflows are lower than expected, a duplicate-looking transaction appears, an important column is missing, or a payment may be pending or delayed.

## High Attention

Could create cash-flow pressure if ignored.

Use when outflows materially exceed inflows, payroll/rent/taxes/debt service appear large relative to inflows, cash balance is declining quickly, revenue appears concentrated, or large obligations cluster together.

## Critical

Requires immediate review or action.

Use only when uploaded data clearly suggests immediate cash-flow pressure, failed payments, overdraft risk, severe negative balance, or a major unresolved obligation.

Do not use Critical when data is merely incomplete.
