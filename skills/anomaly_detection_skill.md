# Anomaly Detection Skill

Use this skill to identify unusual or noteworthy transactions.

## Look For

- Duplicate-looking transactions
- Large single outflows
- Large irregular deposits
- Pending income
- Failed or reversed payments
- Cash balance declines
- Outflows materially exceeding inflows
- Payroll, rent, debt, or tax clusters
- Missing or unclear categories
- Transfer-like transactions that are not labeled clearly

## Key Rule

Flag possible anomalies. Do not remove or correct them silently.
