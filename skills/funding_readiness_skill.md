# Funding Readiness Skill

Use this skill when writing the Funding Readiness Note.

## Procedure

1. Identify any data issues that may affect a funding conversation.
2. Identify cash-flow volatility, negative trends, high debt service, or revenue concentration.
3. Mention documents that may be useful to gather.
4. Keep the framing preparatory.
5. Avoid approval claims or product recommendations.

## Safe Framing

Use wording such as:

"This is not a funding recommendation, but a reviewer may ask about..."
